Google Sheets Setup

Follow these steps once and your product catalog will automatically pull from your Google Sheet every time someone visits the site. No coding needed after setup — just edit the sheet and the website updates itself.

  1. 1
    Create your Google Sheet

    Go to sheets.google.com and create a new spreadsheet.

  2. 2
    Add these exact column headers in Row 1
    ColumnRequired?What to put in it
    nameRequiredProduct name
    descriptionRequiredFull product description
    animalOptionalCattle, Sheep, Goats, Swine…
    styleOptionalButton, Flag, Two-Piece…
    sizeOptionalSmall, Medium, Large…
    colorOptionalYellow, Green, Red…
    priceOptional$1.25/tag (or blank)
    badgeOptionalPopular, New, Sale
    image_urlOptionalPublic image URL
  3. 3
    Publish your sheet to the web

    Google Sheets: File → Share → Publish to web. Choose CSV output and publish.

  4. 4
    Paste the CSV link below and save
    ✓ URL saved! Switch to the Store tab to see your products.
